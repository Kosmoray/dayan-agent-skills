The product model

Four layers. One honest claim.

Skill

Chooses a repeatable workflow and its trigger boundary.

Method

Captures a judgment rule that should survive across tasks.

Fixture

Shows the shape of a useful or unsafe result.

Verifier

Checks the part that should not depend on persuasion.

The promise is deliberately narrow: a check proves the contract it names. It does not magically prove model quality, universal compatibility, factual accuracy, or business outcomes.
